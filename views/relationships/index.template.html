    Upload your snapshot

    Select entities.csv and relations.csv together. JSON is also supported. Optionally add sources.csv, ai-deals.json, or company-aliases.csv.

    Files are validated before replacement. No API key or account is needed.

    Schema & exact file locations
    entities.csv: id,name,layer,kind,parent,region,confidence,as_of,source_urlrelations.csv: source,target,type,confidence,source_url,as_of,source_title
    Only id + name, and source + target + type, are mandatory. JSON accepts arrays or {entities: [...]} and {relations: [...]}. IDs must match exactly. Blank dates stay unknown. Duplicate keys and missing endpoints are rejected.
    Optional ledger: original ai-deals.json, or CSV fields id,announced,deal_type,status,parties,amount_usd,source_url,basis,qualifier. Separate company IDs in parties with semicolons.
    Manual crosswalk: company-aliases.csv with alias,company_id. Leave company_id blank for anything you cannot identify. No fuzzy matches are made.
    Shared snapshot: /data/entities.json, /data/relations.json, /data/sources.csv, /data/deal-ledger.json. /data/shared-snapshot.json records source URLs and SHA-256 checksums.

    Explore company relationships
    1. Start with a company. Search or choose a ranked company. The initial view shows the full network, with all relationship families enabled. Open Filters to narrow it down.
    2. Follow its ties. Click a node to inspect it. Choose “Center neighborhood here” to explore outward. Use Nearby and change Reach to 2 hops for a wider neighborhood. Close the details panel to return the space to the graph.
    3. Read the evidence. Click an arrow or a relationship row for its source, direction, type, confidence, and evidence date.
    4. Find overlapping roles. Enable Investment + compute to show pairs with both investment and compute relationships.
    5. Trace a chain. Open Trace a path, choose two companies, and choose whether to follow arrows. The path uses your current filters.
    6. Use your own data. Open Data & sources and select entities.csv plus relations.csv together. Optional linked tables are kept separate.
    7. Export. Save the visible network as SVG or its relationship rows as CSV. Collapse the controls with the minus button; reopen them with Controls. The offline package needs no installation.
    Degree means distinct counterparties, not relationship rows. Investment + compute overlap alone does not establish circular cash flows.

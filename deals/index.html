How to read this & glossary

Certainty tiers

  • High (solid): Binding deal with company-stated value and source URL.
  • Medium (diagonal hatch): Binding deal with media-reported value.
  • Low (cross-hatch + dashed): Non-binding framework/LOI, or missing source.
  • Snapshots (hollow, bottom lane): Total backlog or program envelopes; excluded from sums and size scaling.
  • Superseded (struck through): Deals replaced or cancelled later.

Glossary

  • Countable binding: Binding deals excluding snapshots/backlogs so totals avoid obvious double-counting.
  • Base term: Initial committed contract length in years (excluding optional extensions).
  • Value basis: What the headline dollar figure measures (contract value, capex, equity, or backlog).
